Do not invent a viewer count or a passing result. Test with people unfamiliar with the script; choose a sufficiently varied group for the intended audience. A passing judgment requires recognizable understanding of the intended general relationship and no recurring misconception about a substantive distinction. A precise name, date, or amount may still need language. If viewers get the wrong general model, revise the objects or behavior before adding labels or polish.
