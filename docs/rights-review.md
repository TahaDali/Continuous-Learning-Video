## Concrete route to close the audio item

The reviewed demo contains 150 narration blocks, 38,057 text characters and 43 minutes 26 seconds of video. Obtain permission covering the existing Edge-generated recordings, or regenerate narration with a provider and voice whose applicable terms cover public hosting and the intended reuse. Keeping Ava through an authorized Azure Speech account is a possible production route to evaluate; it requires that account's applicable terms and authorization and does not establish permission for the old files.

If regenerating, preserve the lesson text, visuals, interface and practice material; measure new word boundaries, rebuild captions and synchronized video, inspect the new delivery, and replace the served media. Keep a private provenance record naming the provider, terms, date, model or voice, and new asset hashes. Do not silently change the user's chosen voice or incur charges. Existing versions remain in Git history and any earlier downloads; changing current files cannot recall those copies. If removing historical recordings is required, prepare a separate branch/tag/cache removal plan and obtain explicit approval before rewriting history.

## Future contributions and releases

For supplied text, images, fonts, code and narration, record the source category, permission or license, applicable attribution and allowed audience before public distribution. Keep private originals and sensitive permission records outside public issues and bundles. An employer reference, fictional label, AI origin or source URL alone does not clear third-party expression. Replace material whose necessary rights cannot be established; do not add an unverified license or author assertion to manufacture clearance.
