# Design decisions

Continuous-Learning-Video combines two methods developed in the earlier Learning Skills project: explain an organization before the employee's work, and animate the causal explanation rather than presenting a sequence of labeled slides.

A substantial parent manuscript precedes adaptation so the final course retains the organization, operating model, worked judgments, hiring evidence and growing responsibility requested by the learner. Distinct evidence categories preserve fact, interpretation, fiction and unknowns. A posting fetch failure must not silently substitute another vacancy.

Sentence-level beat sheets connect meaning, visible state changes and measured narration. Persistent objects, explicit exceptions and source-bound prototypes reduce generic diagram reuse and premature claims of success. New projects have no implemented world; the mechanical example requires an explicit option.

Mechanical and perceptual reviews remain separate. Full media decoding, caption equality and registered geometry are useful screens but do not prove teaching quality. Owner acceptance of the five private cases is recorded in [validation](validation.md) without inventing missing independent listening or learner studies.

The public release consolidates the latest combined workflow into one root skill, replaces the adapted historical case in the payload with a newly authored fictional example, preserves contributor history and prior notices, and makes release file selection explicit. It adds dependency identity, source/output containment and public reporting guidance without changing the subject worlds or the owner's private course audiences.
