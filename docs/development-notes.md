# Development notes

Prepared October 20, 2026, from the installed packages, accessible earlier chat turns, and local validation reports. These are summarized design decisions, not an export of the conversations. Raw transcripts, candidate documents, account identifiers, viewer addresses, private hosting links, generated courses, and narration stems are excluded.

## Company & Role Storyteller

The original teaching approach grew from a company and Salesforce analyst discussion. Its useful pattern was to explain the organization first, follow a fictional customer through its operations, and introduce the employee's decisions only after the business context made them meaningful. Hiring, onboarding, and advancement came later.

In **Analyze Axios Salesforce Analyst Job**, that method became a reusable skill. The historical Axios case preserves narrative continuity and labels its fictional characters, transactions, and measures. It is not a current employer fact sheet, and its learner assumptions must not become another user's biography.

The first realistic internship-posting test in **Analyze U.S. Chamber role** exposed a scope problem. The skill activated, but compressed institutional context, internship details, candidate advice, and career development into one response. The earlier compact scenario checks had not tested the minimal prompt that caused the failure.

The correction made a substantial organization-first Part I the default for a new organization or job link. It preserved explicit requests for a brief answer, all-in-one analysis, narrow factual answers, and continuations. Internship milestones follow the actual appointment duration rather than a permanent employee's year-long review cycle.

The local historical reports record a fresh independent application that produced a focused Part I, plus format and continuity checks. The posting could not be retrieved in that application, and vacancy details remained unverified. Those results support the correction within their scope; they are not an exhaustive fact audit or a guarantee of future output.

## Continuous Learning Videos

The work in **Create Chamber Internship Microlearn** and **Turn new content into motion microlearning** established that playable videos were the intended output. A storyboard or animated page alone did not complete the production request.

Early versions could move objects while still feeling like presentations. Feedback led to persistent visual worlds, progressive construction, camera reframing, object transformations, and transitions caused by existing objects. A later correction made clear that camera travel through sparse graphics was also insufficient: motion must explain the concept.

The sentence-level beat sheet became the editorial bridge between narration and implementation. Each sentence or major idea identifies the concept, metaphor, graphics, behavior, next connection, intended silent meaning, timing, and composition risks. The silent-viewing test evaluates whether the main relationship survives without narration and captions; it does not prove audience comprehension.

Composition feedback required checking intermediate frames, temporary duplicates, full visual extents, caption space, and layering. The starter's geometry audit checks registered axis-aligned bounds and a conservation invariant at output-frame timestamps. Its limits are explicit. It does not certify unregistered graphics, raster effects, or perceptual clarity.

Voice feedback led to Microsoft samples and an Ava selection in the original project. The installed skill preserves that preference as a starting candidate rather than a universal provider rule. Service authorization applies to the current material; past project consent is not blanket permission to upload new documents. The later Chamber revision also confirmed that changing narration requires retiming affected animation and captions.

The review record must distinguish media decoding, caption checks, geometric audits, independent listening, synchronized viewing, and silent visual review. Historical technical passes did not complete full listening or synchronized viewing. User confirmation that audio played was not an independent listening review. A user-accepted release retained those limits.

Hosting and viewer access are separate operations. The reusable skill preserves audience settings and avoids carrying any prior site's identity or viewer addresses into another project.

## What is preserved in this repository

At the initial import, the skill folders were byte-for-byte copies of the installed packages, including their references, UI metadata, executable helpers, canvas example and player. The following checks describe that historical snapshot. Later combined-workflow, renderer and player changes are covered by the [current development status](combined-skill-status.md); they do not update a personal installation automatically.

The skill-creator structural validator passed for both imported skills on October 20, 2026. Repository checks cover local reference resolution, source syntax and JSON parsing, project generation, refusal to overwrite, distinct player progress keys, and the starter's source/beat validation. The optional render check covers the silent example's geometry audit, video stream properties, and full decoding. These checks do not request narration, review an entire course, test learner understanding, or refresh employer research.

The local import check passed across all 27 skill files and eight local reference links. Both project-generation checks passed. The silent demonstration rendered and decoded successfully: 288 frames, zero unresolved geometric findings, and zero declared overlap exemptions. Both skill folders matched the installed versions byte for byte. A pattern scan found no home-directory paths, email addresses, GitHub token patterns, private keys, hosting project IDs, or private course URLs in the prepared repository files; this is a bounded scan rather than a guarantee against every form of sensitive content.

Before publishing publicly, choose a license, review personalized guidance and example provenance, inspect the full history, and try realistic prompts in a fresh environment. Public release remains an owner decision.
