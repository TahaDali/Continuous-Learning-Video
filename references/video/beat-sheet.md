# Visual beat sheet

The sheet is the bridge from script meaning to executable motion. Write it before rendering, then update its timing and wording to match the final narration. Long scripts can group sentences under a beat, but each sentence still needs an explicit cue and educational purpose.

## Fields

| Field | Required meaning |
|---|---|
| Source span / narration | Exact approved sentence or major idea and its source coverage |
| Concept | What the viewer must understand; include the essential qualification |
| Visual metaphor | A concrete, recognizable model that preserves the relationship |
| Graphical elements | Objects, meaningful internal details, paths, boundaries and concise labels |
| Animation behavior | What changes, when, in response to what, and what is conserved |
| Camera | The current focus and the spatial relationship the reframing will reveal |
| Next connection | Which existing object, state or relationship causes or leads into the next idea |
| Silent meaning | What can reasonably be inferred from graphics alone |
| Timing / emphasis | Estimated or measured span; named phrase for an important reveal |
| Composition risk | Crossings, temporary duplicates, small type, masking or caption proximity |

The bundled JSON keeps those fields as `concept`, `metaphor`, `elements`, `behavior`, `camera`, `next`, `silentMeaning`, `timingMethod` and `risks`. `text` must match the narration. Include richer authoring fields when useful.

## Choosing meaningful motion

Prefer visible mechanisms to a label changing:

- A conserved budget: a quantity leaves one container, visibly travels, then enters another. Show the in-flight quantity if displayed totals would otherwise stop balancing. A drifting dollar icon does not teach conservation.
- A conditional gate: attempt a transition with an unmet condition, show it blocked, satisfy the missing condition, then visibly release the same object. Do not teach permissions merely by changing a page layout.
- Migration: preserve source record identity, extract copies, map fields, handle exceptions, and reconcile both counts and value. Count equality alone is not completeness.
- Distinct business measures: let the same deal create separate dated tracks or records for signing, invoicing, payment and recognition. Keep their dates and totals independent.
- Correlation: show compared cohorts, denominators and an alternative explanation. An upward arrow after an intervention can falsely imply causation.

These are examples, not a required icon vocabulary. Use anatomy, mechanisms, maps, people, instruments or materials appropriate to the subject.

## Density and continuity

Keep informational change visible while the explanation advances: construction, comparisons, movement, state changes, causal feedback or measured reveals. A pause for comprehension is purposeful. Arbitrary decorative loops and constant motion are not useful density.

Plan world coordinates, actor identities and camera keyframes before implementing difficult transitions. Keep previous context as a smaller anchor when it remains relevant. Use a pan, zoom or transformation to expose a relationship; do not attach a scene replacement to every narration block. Continuous storytelling does not require one enormous unbroken video across separate lessons.

Use short object labels, units and symbols where needed. Full sentences belong primarily in optional captions or transcripts. Necessary terminology, legends and quantitative values are compatible with minimal text.

## Silent review

Export excerpts with neither audio nor subtitle streams. Review at least one explanation-heavy passage and one difficult transition per distinct visual mechanism, then inspect the complete lessons as appropriate. Ask what the objects do and what can be inferred without seeing the script. A report should distinguish the producer's own visual inspection from independent learner comprehension testing.

Do not turn the silent test into a demand that graphics convey every date, proper noun or legal qualification. The test is whether the core mechanism and relationship survive without verbal scaffolding.
